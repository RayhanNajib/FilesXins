01

UM-MART {{icon:lucide:circle-check}} In production

Food & grocery e-commerce platform — campus market

Problem

A campus market selling groceries had no online ordering, no stock visibility for staff, and no transaction records — every order was handled by hand.

What I built

  • Product catalogue with categories, live search and filter combinations
  • Persistent cart and a complete checkout flow
  • Four payment methods — OVO, GoPay, DANA and card
  • Printable invoices and per-customer transaction history
  • Admin dashboard: statistics, charts, banner & voucher CRUD, Excel export
  • Dual authentication — password and Google Sign-In

Result

11 tables and a working multi-role system delivered end to end, with 2 works registered as intellectual property. The full front end is deployed as a live demo.

02

Klinik Pratama UM Laravel 12

Clinic management system with role-based access control

Problem

A primary clinic ran schedules, patient records, billing and medical certificates on paper, with no consistent access control between administrative staff, doctors and patients.

What I built

  • Three role dashboards — admin, doctor and patient — from one codebase
  • Admin: doctor, patient and schedule CRUD, plus cashiering and payment reports
  • Doctor: appointment queue, diagnosis entry, printable medical certificates
  • Patient: schedule browsing, appointment booking and cancellation, history
  • Accounting and payment records over a normalised MySQL schema

Result

16 tables, 6 users and 3 doctor profiles modelled with full role separation. A working replacement for the paper process, with printable output at every step.

Public information portal — Desa Nanggungan, Kayen Kidul
