01. About me

I'm a Information Technology student at Universitas Negeri Malang who likes the part of software where the pixels finally move. Most of what I build is PHP/MySQL on the back end with hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript on the front — no framework between me and the DOM.

I've shipped a full grocery e-commerce platform (catalog, cart, four payment flows, admin dashboard with charts and Excel export) and a clinic management system in Laravel with admin / doctor / patient roles and printable medical records. I also built the village portal for Desa Nanggungan, which is live on the public internet right now.

Away from the keyboard I'm either gaming or tinkering in Blender — modelling, rigging, and rendering the 3D ideas that don't fit inside a browser tab. I also do UI/UX design in Figma, which is where most of my web work starts.

Currently: open to freelance web work & internships.

02. Projects

01

UM-MART Live

Food & grocery e-commerce platform

A complete online grocery store for a campus market. Product catalog with categories and filters, live search, a persistent cart, checkout, four payment flows (OVO, GoPay, DANA, card), printable invoices and a full admin dashboard with statistics charts, banner and voucher management, plus Excel export. Accounts support both password login and Google Sign-In.

02

Klinik Pratama UM Laravel

Clinic management system with role-based access

A clinic back office built on Laravel 12 with three separate experiences: an admin managing doctors, patients, schedules and cashiering; a doctor taking appointments, writing diagnoses and issuing printable medical certificates; and a patient portal for browsing schedules, booking appointments and viewing history. Payments, schedules and records all live in a normalised MySQL schema.

  • Laravel 12
  • PHP 8.2+
  • MySQL
  • Blade
  • Vite
  • RBAC

Official village portal · Desa Nanggungan, Kayen Kidul
